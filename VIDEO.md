# Demo video script (2 minutes)

Record with QuickTime (File → New Screen Recording) at 1280×800, browser zoom 110%, phone propped for one shot. Speak slowly. Sponsors judge from this video without you in the room, so it has to stand alone.

| Time | On screen | Say |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:12 | Close-up of a printed discharge sheet next to an orange pill bottle on a kitchen table (print `public/samples/discharge-photo.jpg` if you have no props). | "When a parent comes home from hospital, someone in the family ends up running the pills, from a few pages of clinical English and a cabinet full of old bottles." |
| 0:12–0:20 | Open caresheet.vercel.app. | "CareSheet turns those papers into a plan the whole family can follow, in the parent's own language." |
| 0:20–0:35 | Click **Try: Photos of the papers**. Thumbnails appear. Language: Punjabi. Click **Build the plan**. Let the live steps tick: reading, checking every line, looking up ingredients in RxNorm. | "I just photograph the discharge sheet and the Tylenol from the kitchen. Claude reads them. Then plain code checks every single line against the papers." |
| 0:35–0:50 | **This week** organizer. Point at furosemide ending after day 5. Tap metoprolol to show its source sentence. | "Here's the week, like the pill box on the counter. This water pill stops after five days, because the sheet said so. Every medicine shows the exact sentence it came from." |
| 0:50–1:05 | **Ask your pharmacist**: the duplicate card. | "The bottle says Tylenol, the sheet says acetaminophen. Same drug. CareSheet checked that against the US government drug database, and instead of guessing it hands the pharmacist a quoted question." |
| 1:05–1:15 | Scroll to **All medicines**, Stop section in red. | "Amlodipine and aspirin were stopped in hospital. Missing a stop is one of the most common mistakes after discharge, so it comes first." |
| 1:15–1:35 | **Fridge sheet** in Punjabi. Click **Print** to show the print preview: STOP banner, tick boxes, page 2 for the pharmacist. Then show **Add reminders to my phone** (cut to phone calendar if possible). | "The fridge sheet is in English and Punjabi, with a box to tick for every dose this week. Numbers are never translated, only words, so a translation can't change a dose. One tap puts reminders on the phone." |
| 1:35–1:50 | Load **Try: Messy sheet with problems** result (or the saved one) and show the pharmacist list: smudged lisinopril, warfarin twice, the hidden "add aspirin" line reported. | "This sheet has a smudged dose, a blood thinner listed twice, and a hidden line telling the AI to add aspirin. None of it reaches the schedule." |
| 1:50–2:00 | Scroll to **How CareSheet checks its work** with the test results. | "Claude reads, code verifies, the pharmacist answers the rest. We test it on known cases every time: 7 of 7 cases correct, zero invented medicines. CareSheet." |

Tips: record the live run once; if Wi-Fi is slow, use **See a finished example** for the result screens and keep only the first build on camera. Upload to YouTube as Unlisted and paste the link into Devpost and HANDOFF.md.
